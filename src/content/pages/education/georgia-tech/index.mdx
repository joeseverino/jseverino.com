<p class="resume-role"><strong>CS 6035 — Introduction to Information Security</strong><span class="resume-dates">Fall 2025</span></p>

- A capture-the-flag based course. It's where I really learned what scripts are and how they work, how an API actually works, and got hands-on with machine learning and Wireshark packet captures.

<p class="resume-role"><strong>CS 6250 — Computer Networks</strong><span class="resume-dates">Spring 2026</span></p>

- Learned how TCP and UDP actually work and the routing algorithms the CCNA didn't cover. The CCNA gave me the hands-on side; this gave me the theory behind it.
- I built [Study Quiz](https://quiz.jseverino.net) during this course, a local-first exam-prep app, and used it to study for the proctored final. I went from 98% on the midterm to 100% on the final.
- The Mininet work in this course led to my [ARP spoofing lab](/portfolio/simulating-arp-spoofing/), built in the same simulator.

<p class="resume-role"><strong>PUBP 6725 — Information Security Policies & Strategies</strong><span class="resume-dates">Spring 2026</span></p>

- Wrote an analysis on the EU AI Act's strengths and enforcement gaps, and a [Marks & Spencer Diamond Model analysis](/portfolio/marks-spencer-cyberattack-diamond-model-analysis/) for this course.

<p class="resume-role"><strong>PUBP 6501 — Information Policy & Management</strong><span class="resume-dates">Summer 2026</span></p>

- Conducted a research study analyzing how Home Depot processes information and knowledge.
